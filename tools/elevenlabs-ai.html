Executive Benchmark Summary

ElevenLabs is purpose-built for Content creators, audiobook publishers, game studios, and low-latency conversational voice AI apps. Its entry-level paid plan starts at $5/month and includes a free tier for testing before upgrading.

✔ Key Strengths (Pros)

  • New Eleven v4 ($0.08/1K chars) and Eleven v4 Turbo ($0.04/1K chars, ~100ms latency) deliver studio-grade emotive speech
  • Starter starts at $5/mo annual ($6/mo monthly) for 30,000 credits, commercial rights, Dubbing Studio, and instant voice cloning
  • Creator ($18.33/mo annual / $22 monthly, 121K credits) unlocks Pro Voice Cloning; Pro ($82.50/mo annual / $99 monthly) adds 600K credits & 44.1kHz PCM API

⚠ Limitations (Cons)

  • Free tier (10,000 credits/mo) excludes commercial usage rights
  • Dubbing and complex multi-speaker workflows consume credits faster than standard TTS
