Executive Benchmark Summary

Suno is purpose-built for Musicians, video creators, podcasters, and indie studios generating original tracks and stems. Its entry-level paid plan starts at $8/month and includes a free tier for testing before upgrading.

✔ Key Strengths (Pros)

  • Free tier grants 50 daily credits (~10 songs/day); Pro starts at $8/mo annual ($10/mo monthly) for 2,500 credits
  • Premier ($24/mo annual / $30/mo monthly) unlocks 10,000 credits, 60 downloads/mo, and Suno Studio DAW
  • Paid tiers include commercial usage rights and multi-track stem separation

⚠ Limitations (Cons)

  • No official self-serve public developer API available
  • Monthly track download caps apply (20 on Pro, 60 on Premier, 120 on Platinum)
